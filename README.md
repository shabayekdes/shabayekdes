## Engineering principles

- **Make trade-offs explicit.** Document why a decision was made and what it costs.
- **Prefer maintainable systems.** Optimize for clarity and safe change, not cleverness.
- **Treat reliability and security as product features.**
- **Verify what is implemented.** Keep shipped capabilities separate from proposals and roadmap items.
- **Build for the people who operate the system.** Good defaults, useful logs, predictable deployments, and actionable documentation matter.
